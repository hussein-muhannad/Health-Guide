Anxiety

What is Anxiety?

Anxiety is a feeling of fear, worry, or uneasiness. It is a normal response to stressful situations, but it can become a health problem when it is excessive or continues for a long time.

Symptoms

Common symptoms may include excessive worry, restlessness, difficulty concentrating, problems sleeping, rapid heartbeat, sweating, and feeling tense.

Causes and Risk Factors

Anxiety can be related to stressful life experiences, family history, changes in the brain, and certain medical conditions. Different factors can affect each person differently.

Diagnosis

A healthcare professional can assess symptoms, medical history, and how anxiety affects daily life. There is no single laboratory test used to diagnose most anxiety disorders.

Treatment

Treatment may include psychological therapies, such as cognitive behavioral therapy, medicines, or a combination of treatments depending on the person's condition.

Prevention and Management

Helpful steps can include regular physical activity, getting enough sleep, managing stress, maintaining social connections, and seeking professional help when symptoms become difficult to manage.

When to Seek Help

If anxiety is interfering with daily activities, relationships, study, work, or sleep, it is important to speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
